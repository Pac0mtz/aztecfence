# Aztec Fence — Keyword Research & Human Content Guide

Keyword research paired with **ready-to-use, human-written copy** for each page. The tone is
what a 30-year, family-run Northern Illinois fence company would actually say: plain, warm,
specific, and confident — never keyword-stuffed. Keywords are woven in naturally.

**Voice rules**
- Talk to a homeowner or facility manager, not a search engine.
- Lead with the customer's problem/benefit, then the fence.
- Use real local place names (Lake County, Round Lake, Gurnee, Libertyville…) sparingly and naturally.
- One clear next step everywhere: call or get a free quote.

## Long-tail & local content ideas (for blog / FAQ / service-area pages)

These capture high-intent, low-competition local searches:

- "How much does a fence cost in Lake County, IL?"
- "Do I need a permit for a fence in [Round Lake / Gurnee / Libertyville]?"
- "Best fence for Illinois winters"
- "Wood vs. vinyl privacy fence: which lasts longer?"
- "How tall can my fence be in [town]?"
- Per-town service-area pages: "Fence installation in Gurnee, IL", "…in Libertyville, IL", etc.
  (Use the service-area list already on the home page as the page set — strong local-SEO play.)

**Golden rule for all of it:** write the answer a real customer is asking for, in plain English.
The keywords take care of themselves when the content is genuinely helpful.
